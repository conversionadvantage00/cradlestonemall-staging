Good to know

Can't find what you're looking for? Call centre management on 010 020 1234.

What are your trading hours?

Monday to Saturday 09:00 – 18:00, and Sunday and public holidays 09:00 – 17:00. Restaurants trade later and individual stores may vary.

Is parking free?

Yes. Parking is free all day, every day. The Woolworths entrance is in the basement parking.

How do I get the free WiFi?

Connect to "@Smart Mall Free WiFi", open the landing page in your browser, complete the fields and unlock 10GB of free data a day in the SmartMall App.

Where can I charge my electric car?

The EV charging unit is at Entrance 1. It offers AC and DC charging, is powered by Jaguar and suits all electric cars.

Where do I get a wheelchair or gift card?

Both are at the Guest Relations Desk at Entrance 5. Gift cards are sold Monday to Sunday, 09:00 – 16:00.
