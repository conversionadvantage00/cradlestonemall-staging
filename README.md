# Cradlestone Mall: website mockup v1

Open `index.html` in any browser. Built on the same system as the Greenacres/Greenstone mockups (archived in `../_archive/`).

## Pages
- `index.html`: homepage
- `stores.html`: store directory (search, 12 categories, Upper/Lower filter, tap-to-call list, store pop-up with special hours)

## Brand (from their current site)
Indigo `#250C84` (primary), sunny yellow `#FFBE3E`, teal `#2BA99D`, orange `#F07A2C`; logo gold `#C3A13F` + charcoal. Their cut-out-people-on-colour-blocks artwork is used for the hero, tiles and banners.

## Real content used
Trading hours + restaurant exceptions, 153 stores (shop no., level, phone), Dino World Family Festival (27 Nov 2026 – 10 Jan 2027) + T&Cs, store promo artwork, SmartMall 10GB free WiFi steps, EV charging (Entrance 1), Guest Relations (Entrance 5), free parking, Ster Kinekor booking link, management contacts, ownership (Sasol & Redefine), managed by Broll.
